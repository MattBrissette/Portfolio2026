Lab

Larus

Trade show conversations are short, and complex enterprise software is hard to explain in a few minutes. As design engineer, I designed and built an interactive 3D exhibition experience from scratch, launched live alongside Larus's physical booth on the floor at CANSEC, Canada's largest defence and security trade show.

The build combines storytelling, product demos, downloadable resources, and lead capture into one immersive experience, developed with AI-assisted tooling (Gemini + Claude) and built to be re-skinned for future shows.

mattbrissette.github.io/3d_booth

Loam

A spatial search engine built for discovery, not scrolling. As design engineer, I led the concept, interaction design, and full Three.js/WebGL build, transforming search results into an explorable 3D environment where unexpected connections emerge.

Powered by live data from six sources, users explore clusters, expand searches, pin discoveries, and export findings as Markdown notes.

Built with Three.js, WebGL, GLSL, GSAP, spatial algorithms, and AI-assisted development (Gemini + Claude), supporting thousands of entities in real time.

About

I'm a UX and Digital Experience Designer with 10 years of experience, based in Gatineau, Quebec. I design intuitive digital experiences, websites, applications, and interfaces for B2B and B2C companies, turning complex ideas and business needs into clear, user-friendly solutions.

My process spans UX, information architecture, wireframing, prototyping, and visual design. I enjoy understanding client needs, exploring solutions, presenting ideas, and working closely with developers to bring projects from concept to completion.

I'm curious about new technologies and constantly exploring how AI, creative coding, and tools like Claude and Figma can improve the design process and create better digital experiences. My work has been recognized by Awwwards and CSS Design Awards. I'm fluent in both English et Français :)
